Copy-Transform-Paste: Zero-Shot Object-Object Alignment Guided by Vision-Language and Geometric Constraints

Rotem Gatenyo, Ohad Fried

A zero-shot method for aligning two 3D meshes from a natural-language description of their spatial relationship. The approach optimizes pose and scale with differentiable rendering and vision-language guidance, while geometric constraints encourage contact and reduce interpenetration.

Distinct Connectivity Patterns Between Perception and Attention-Related Brain Networks Characterize Dyslexia: Machine Learning Applied to Resting-State fMRI

Nikolay Taran, Rotem Gatenyo, Emmanuelle Hadjadj, Rola Farah, Tzipi Horowitz-Kraus

A machine learning study of resting-state functional connectivity in children with and without dyslexia. Connectivity patterns between brain networks related to perception and attention were used to investigate automated classification and the neural characteristics associated with dyslexia.
