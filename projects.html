Physical Design / SolidWorks / 2022

Maze Zaz — Midburn Interactive Installation

Maze Zaz was a collaborative interactive installation built for Midburn. I began the project with minimal SolidWorks experience from my undergraduate studies and no background in designing physical parts, so I taught myself the required tools and fabrication principles along the way. I translated the team's physical concept into manufacturable components, developed assembly visualizations, and prepared dimensioned drawings for CNC fabrication and manual cutting.

  • Role CAD design and fabrication planning
  • Tools SolidWorks, CNC routing, woodworking
